Quarterly Business Review

Prepared by the Finance team for the board meeting of 14 January 2026.

# 1\. Summary

Revenue grew in every region. The North region stayed the largest; the West region grew fastest in Q4.

- Total revenue reached USD 1,238 thousand, up 14% year over year.
- Gross margin improved from 41% to 44%.
- Headcount stayed flat at 86.

# 3\. Method

Year-over-year growth is computed as

- Collect booked revenue from the ledger.
- Convert to USD at the month-end rate.
- Sum by region and quarter.
