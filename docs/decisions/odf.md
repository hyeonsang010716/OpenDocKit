# OpenDocument decisions (ODT, ODS, ODP)

Deliberate decisions of `extractors/odf.py`. Common rules (limits, tables, pictures, wording of messages) are in [core.md](core.md) and [extractors.md](extractors.md); the sheet-splitting rules reused here are in [spreadsheet-tables.md](spreadsheet-tables.md).

All tests are in `test_odf.py`.

## Package and structure

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ODF is read with `zipfile` + `lxml` only: `content.xml` (body, automatic styles), `styles.xml` (named styles, list styles, master pages), `meta.xml` (title, author), `META-INF/manifest.xml` (encryption). One renderer serves the three formats | No new dependency (odfpy builds a full DOM and has no bounds on repeat counts). Paragraphs, lists, tables, frames and notes are the same elements in text documents, slides and — for cell text — sheets | `TestTextDocument::test_format_is_detected_and_title_comes_from_meta`, `TestSpreadsheet::test_one_sheet_section_per_table_with_displayed_cell_text`, `TestPresentation::test_one_slide_section_per_page_with_the_title_from_the_title_placeholder` |
| A manifest that contains `encryption-data` is `EncryptedDocumentError`. A missing, unreadable or body-less `content.xml` is `CorruptedDocumentError` with one fixed message. A broken `styles.xml` keeps the body with a warning (lists fall back to bullets, headers/footers are lost); a broken `meta.xml`, a missing picture or a broken sub-document costs only itself. Parts are read with a size cap (`max_uncompressed_bytes`) | The parts of an encrypted package are ciphertext, so without the check the file was reported as corrupted. A document with a sound body must not be rejected for one part outside it (the HWPX rule) | `TestBrokenParts::test_encrypted_package_is_reported_as_password_protected`, `test_broken_content_part_is_a_corrupted_document_with_a_fixed_message`, `test_broken_styles_part_keeps_the_body_with_a_warning`, `test_broken_meta_and_missing_picture_cost_only_themselves` |
| The body element decides what is rendered (`office:text`, `office:spreadsheet`, `office:presentation`), the detected kind only says which to look for first | A package whose mimetype and body disagree still has readable content | `TestBrokenParts::test_body_decides_when_the_mimetype_names_another_kind` |
| White space in XML text is collapsed to one space; real spaces, tabs and line breaks come from `text:s`, `text:tab`, `text:line-break` (`text:s` writes at most 8 spaces) | ODF defines it so. Indented XML otherwise became line breaks inside a sentence. Spaces are collapsed by the converter anyway, so `text:c="1000000000"` must not allocate | `TestTextDocument::test_spaces_tabs_and_line_breaks_come_from_their_elements_not_from_xml_white_space`, `TestHostileInput::test_style_that_inherits_from_itself_and_absurd_numbers_do_not_hang` |
| Rendering recurses at most 40 levels (`_MAX_DEPTH`: sections, lists, tables in cells, text boxes, groups, spans); deeper content is kept as plain text. Rows are walked with a stack. Style inheritance is followed 16 styles at most. Nesting beyond the XML parser's own limit is a corrupted document | A crafted file ended in a RecursionError; a style naming itself as parent never ended | `TestHostileInput::test_deeply_nested_elements_keep_their_text_without_recursing`, `test_nesting_beyond_the_parser_limit_fails_cleanly`, `test_style_that_inherits_from_itself_and_absurd_numbers_do_not_hang` |
| Entities are not resolved and nothing is fetched: pictures come only from parts of the package (a link with a scheme, an absolute path or `..` is ignored) or from `office:binary-data`. A picture part is decompressed once however often it is shown | Entity bombs, SSRF and path tricks. One 40 MB picture shown 80 times was decompressed 80 times in HWPX before the same rule | `TestHostileInput::test_entities_are_not_expanded`, `test_picture_link_out_of_the_package_is_not_followed`, `test_picture_shown_many_times_is_read_once` |
| The character limit is checked per top-level block of a text document (looking through sections and indexes, so a document wrapped in one section still stops early), per cell of a top-level table (`TableBudget`), per sheet row and per slide. Footnote and comment text is not counted while reading (undercounting is the safe side) | Same contract as the other extractors: stop reading once the result would be rejected | `TestCharacterLimit` (5 tests) |

## Text (all three formats)

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `text:h` is a heading by `text:outline-level` (1–6). A `text:p` in the 'Title' style or a built-in 'Heading n' style (`Heading_20_n`, or a style derived from one) is a heading too. Inside a table cell, a note or a slide title a heading is plain text. Known accepted gap: chapter numbers that LibreOffice generates from the outline numbering are not in the text and are not reproduced | LibreOffice writes the document title as a `text:p`, and its HTML import wrote `<h1>` as a `text:p` in a style derived from 'Heading 1'; the DOCX original gives `#` for both. A `#` inside a cell is not a heading | `TestTextDocument::test_headings_follow_the_outline_level_and_the_title_style`, `test_paragraph_in_a_heading_style_is_a_heading` |
| Whether a list level is bulleted, numbered or unlabelled comes from the list style (`text:list-level-style-bullet` / `-number`; a number style with an empty `style:num-format` shows no label and does not indent the level below). A nested list uses the style of the outermost list unless it names one; an unknown style means bullets. Numbers are counted here: a new `text:list` starts again unless `text:continue-numbering` / `text:continue-list` is set, `text:start-value` is honoured, Arabic numbers are always written `1.`, other shapes (a, i, 가, ①, 一 …) keep the file's prefix and suffix and take the `- 가.` form below the top level (`_shared.list_item_prefix`). A list item that holds only a nested list gets no line of its own. Inside a table cell levels are marked with `cell_level_mark` | ODF stores no label text, only the style. Impress writes unbulleted text-box text as a list level without a label and second-level bullets as a list inside an item without a paragraph — read literally these became `- ` lines and empty bullets | `TestTextDocument::test_bulleted_and_numbered_lists_are_told_apart_by_the_list_style`, `test_nested_list_levels_are_indented_and_numbered_per_level`, `test_numbering_continues_across_lists_only_when_the_file_says_so`, `TestPresentation::test_bullet_levels_are_nested_lists`, `test_list_level_without_a_label_is_a_paragraph_and_does_not_indent_the_level_below` |
| Bold and italic are read from text (character) styles of spans, automatic or named, with inheritance from parent styles and enclosing spans (a span may turn bold off). For Hangul, kana and Han text the `style:font-weight-asian` / `style:font-style-asian` properties decide when present. Marks never wrap white space, adjacent runs with the same emphasis are merged (white space between them included), text without a letter or digit is not marked, and headings get no emphasis. Known accepted gap: a paragraph that is bold only through its paragraph style is not marked | Paragraph-level bold is styling (table headers, captions), not emphasis. LibreOffice set only the Asian property on a bold Korean word, so reading `fo:font-weight` alone lost emphasis exactly in Korean documents | `TestInlineFormatting::test_bold_and_italic_spans_become_markdown_emphasis`, `test_emphasis_marks_never_wrap_white_space_and_adjacent_runs_merge`, `test_span_that_turns_bold_off_inside_a_bold_span_is_plain`, `test_bold_korean_text_is_read_from_the_asian_font_properties`, `test_keep_emphasis_off_flattens_emphasis`, `test_no_emphasis_inside_headings` |
| A paragraph in a table cell whose text is emphasized as a whole is left plain; emphasis that sets part of the cell text apart is kept | Header rows and label columns are bold in most documents; marking them put `**` around every header cell (Impress tables carry a character style on every run) | `TestInlineFormatting::test_cell_that_is_bold_as_a_whole_is_left_plain` |
| `text:a` becomes `[text](address)` only for http, https and mailto addresses; spaces, parentheses and angle brackets in the address are percent-encoded, addresses over 2,000 characters are dropped. A link whose text is its address is written once. Bookmarks and other targets keep the text only | Internal targets (`#1.개요\|outline`) mean nothing outside the document; `[https://x](https://x)` doubles the tokens | `TestInlineFormatting::test_hyperlink_becomes_a_markdown_link_only_for_web_and_mail_targets`, `test_link_whose_text_is_its_address_is_written_once`, `test_bold_text_inside_a_link_keeps_both` |
| Superscript/subscript (`style:text-position`) and strikethrough (`style:text-line-through-style`) use the shared notation (`10^6`, `H_2O`, `~~text~~`) | `10⁶` must not read `106`; a struck price must not read as valid (the rules of core.md) | `TestInlineFormatting::test_superscript_and_strikethrough_use_the_shared_notation` |
| Tracked changes: deleted text (stored in `text:tracked-changes`) is left out, inserted text is kept | Both the old and the new value read as valid (same direction as DOCX `w:del`) | `TestTextDocument::test_tracked_deletion_is_left_out_and_insertion_kept` |
| `text:note` is `[^n]` with `[^n]: text` at the end, numbered in the order of reference (footnotes and endnotes together); with `include_footnotes` off the mark is left out too. A table inside a note is one line (`note_table`). Comments (`office:annotation`) are `> Comment(author): text` lines at the end of the document, slide or sheet (with the cell). Ruby text is `base(annotation)` | The forms the DOCX, PPTX, XLSX and HWP extractors use | `TestTextDocument::test_footnotes_and_endnotes_are_numbered_in_order_with_definitions_at_the_end`, `test_comment_is_written_at_the_end`, `test_ruby_text_follows_its_base_in_parentheses`, `TestSpreadsheet::test_cell_comment_and_chart_title_follow_the_cells` |
| Tables of text documents and slides go through `TableGrid` with the span counts of the file; `table:covered-table-cell` only takes up a position; a table inside a cell is handed to the outer grid (`embed`). A cell on a position already covered moves to the next free one (`place_or_next`) | The shared merged-cell rules (vertical merge repeated, horizontal merge → HTML). Files without their covered cells lost cell text | `TestTextDocument::test_merged_cells_repeat_their_value_on_every_row`, `test_horizontally_merged_cell_makes_an_html_table`, `test_nested_table_keeps_its_rows_and_columns` |
| The children of a `draw:frame` are alternatives: the first that gives content is used (text box, table, embedded object, picture). `presentation:placeholder="true"` frames are nothing. Sections, text boxes and index bodies are containers of body text; hidden sections (`text:display="none"`) and index settings are left out | A slide table carries a preview picture and a chart a replacement picture (StarView metafiles the converter cannot decode): using both gave the content twice plus an 'unreadable image' warning. Layout prompts are not content | `TestPresentation::test_table_and_picture_on_a_slide`, `test_empty_layout_placeholders_are_left_out`, `TestObjects::test_chart_gives_its_title_and_value_table`, `TestTextDocument::test_text_box_section_and_table_of_contents_keep_their_text` |
| An embedded chart is `[Chart] title` + the chart's own copy of its values (`table:table` in the sub-document, corner cell 'Category'), the shape the PPTX extractor writes. In a sheet only the title is written. An embedded formula is `$LaTeX$` from its presentation MathML (same rules as `html._mathml_latex`) and stays inside its sentence; when the MathML gives nothing, the StarMath annotation is written between `$` as it is. Known accepted gap: StarMath is not LaTeX. Other embedded objects fall back to their replacement picture | The values of a sheet chart are the cells of that sheet. LibreOffice turns a DOCX equation into a Math object; without this the formula of the sample report was an unreadable picture | `TestObjects` (3 tests), `TestSpreadsheet::test_cell_comment_and_chart_title_follow_the_cells` |
| Headers and footers (master pages of `styles.xml`) are written only with `include_headers_footers`: each distinct text once, headers before the body and footers after it. Page-number and page-count fields are never written (they hold the number cached at the last save) | Same option as DOCX/HWPX; ten master pages with the same footer must not repeat it | `TestTextDocument::test_headers_and_footers_only_with_the_option` |

## Spreadsheet

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cell text is the displayed text (`text:p`), not the stored value: number formats are not interpreted. A cell with a value but no `text:p` (other writers) gets the stored value in a plain form (number, percent, ISO date, `HH:MM:SS`, TRUE/FALSE). Emphasis and links are not kept in cells; superscripts and strikethrough are | LibreOffice stores what the cell showed ('$120k', '46.4%', '2025-03-14'), which is exactly what the XLSX extractor reconstructs from number formats. Markdown marks in cells would break header and number detection of the sheet splitting | `TestSpreadsheet::test_one_sheet_section_per_table_with_displayed_cell_text`, `test_cell_without_displayed_text_falls_back_to_its_stored_value` |
| Splitting a sheet into tables, title rows, note rows and merge filling use the spreadsheet extractor's helpers as they are (`spreadsheet._collect_rows`, `_append_sheet`, `_warn_once`; private names imported, nothing changed there). Rows are produced the way `spreadsheet._xml_sheet_rows` does: a gap of empty rows is one empty row, a vertical merge is repeated in the rows below that have content, a horizontal merge in a row with text to its left is filled after splitting (`_fill_across`) | An ODS and the XLSX it was saved from must come out the same; a second copy of those rules would drift. Exception to the dependency direction of core.md, like DOCX altChunk → html | `TestSpreadsheet::test_two_tables_on_one_sheet_are_split_like_xlsx`, `test_vertically_merged_cell_is_repeated_on_the_rows_below`, `test_horizontal_merge_in_a_data_row_fills_the_named_columns` |
| `table:number-columns-repeated` / `number-rows-repeated` on empty cells and rows only move the position and cost nothing. A repeated cell with text is repeated up to the column limit; a repeated row with text is written at most 10,000 times (`_MAX_REPEAT`) and all repeats of a document may add at most 5 million characters (`_REPEAT_BUDGET`), with a warning naming the sheet when cut. Columns beyond 16,384 are never materialized whatever `max_sheet_cols` says. Text tables use the same bounds | Every LibreOffice sheet ends with a row repeated about a million times holding 16,384 repeated cells. A row with text and `number-rows-repeated="1000000000"` is a few bytes that became gigabytes when `max_chars` is None. Known accepted gap: more than 10,000 identical consecutive rows are cut (with the warning) | `TestSpreadsheet::test_trailing_repeats_of_empty_rows_and_cells_cost_nothing`, `test_repeated_cells_and_rows_with_text_are_expanded`, `TestHostileInput::test_repeated_rows_with_text_are_cut_short_with_a_warning`, `test_repeated_cell_with_text_stops_at_the_column_limit`, `test_cell_far_to_the_right_and_far_down_does_not_build_an_empty_grid`, `test_huge_repeat_counts_on_empty_cells_and_rows_in_a_text_table_are_ignored`, `test_large_sheet_converts_in_linear_time` |
| A sheet whose table style has `table:display="false"` is hidden: skipped with `Hidden sheet 'name' was excluded.` unless `include_hidden_sheets`. `max_sheet_cols` applies with the XLSX warning. Charts (`[Chart] title`) and pictures follow the cells, then the cell comments | The XLSX behaviour and wording | `TestSpreadsheet::test_hidden_sheet_is_skipped_with_a_warning_unless_included`, `test_columns_beyond_the_limit_are_excluded_with_a_warning`, `test_cell_comment_and_chart_title_follow_the_cells` |

## Presentation

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One SLIDE section per `draw:page`. The title is the text of the first `presentation:class="title"` frame and is not repeated in the slide text; the other shapes follow in document order (frames, shapes with text, groups). Header, footer, date and page-number placeholders are left out. A slide whose drawing-page style has `presentation:visibility="hidden"` gets ` (hidden)` after its title, as in PPTX. Known accepted gap: text that sits only on the master page is not read | The PPTX extractor's shape of a slide | `TestPresentation::test_one_slide_section_per_page_with_the_title_from_the_title_placeholder`, `test_hidden_slide_is_marked_in_its_title` |
| Speaker notes (`presentation:notes`) are `> Speaker notes: …` after the slide text when `include_speaker_notes`: the notes placeholder and text boxes added to the notes page; the slide thumbnail and page number are not notes. List levels inside notes are marked with `cell_level_mark`, without bullets or numbers | Same form and same accepted gap as PPTX notes (leading spaces of a quote line are lost) | `TestPresentation::test_speaker_notes_use_the_pptx_form_and_follow_the_option` |
