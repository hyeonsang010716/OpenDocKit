# HWP / HWPX decisions

Deliberate decisions of the HWP and HWPX extractors (`hwp.py`, `hwpx.py`; the paragraph rules both follow are in `_hangul.py`). Split off from [extractors.md](extractors.md); the rule is the same:
do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change its pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.

## HWP / HWPX

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A paragraph with an outline head (HWP PARA_SHAPE head kind 1, HWPX `hh:heading type="OUTLINE"`) in the top-level body is a heading of its outline level + 1, whatever its style and whatever its text says, unless it is the sentence of a numbered clause (`_outline_title`: `다` after the stems and endings `하 같 있 없 이 니 았 었 였 겠 않` (`…아니하다`, `…같다`, `…있다`) or a syllable closed by ㅆ (`…했다`, `…됐다`), or a Korean sentence's `다.` of any length (`제3조를 준용한다.`), or, in three words or more, after a syllable closed by ㄴ, the plain-style `…한다`, `…따른다`, `…본다`, or another full stop; a colon, semicolon or dash after it is looked past. Not `캐나다`, `바다`, whose `다` follows an open syllable, not `우간다`, `미래가 온다`, `Appendix A.` (fewer words), not a question mark, which ends FAQ titles, and not the 개조식 endings `…함`, `…임`, `…것`); the guards of the size headings do not apply, as the head is the author's own mark of a title (`1. 캐나다`, `Ⅰ. 총칙(개정 2024. 1. 1.)`, `2024년 12월`, `왜 바다인가?` stay headings; known accepted gaps: a title ending like a sentence, `아는 것이 힘이다`, `1인 가구가 늘어난다`, is a numbered paragraph, and a clause in 개조식 or of two words, `을은 책임을 짐`, `갑이 정한다`, is a heading), and it may be up to 120 characters like an outline style's; when the style is a 개요 style too, the style gives the level as before, with the guards of an outline style (`_hangul.decorated_text`). A paragraph whose outline head makes no heading stays a numbered paragraph. In a cell, text box, caption or header an outline head stays a number, and no paragraph of a footnote or endnote is a heading, by style or by head | Hangul builds the document outline and the table of contents from the head, and a real Hangul sample (`examples/samples/numbered-outline.hwpx`) puts outline heads on 바탕글 paragraphs: they came out as a numbered list (`1. 개요1`, `  - 가. 개요1-1`) with no heading. A heading in a note wrote `[^1]: # 1. 항목`, which is not a heading and breaks the note. Contracts and regulations number their clauses with outline heads: without the text guards every clause became a heading (`# 1. 갑은 을에게 계약금을 … 지급한다.`, `## 가. 다만, … 아니하다.`), and one such clause turned off the size headings of the whole document (they are no longer turned off by any outline heading: the row below) | `test_hwp_size_headings.py::TestOutlineHeads` (including `test_no_heading_in_a_note_or_text_box`, `test_numbered_clause_sentences_stay_numbered_paragraphs`), `TestOutlineHeads::test_titles_with_outline_heads_stay_headings` |
| A top-level body paragraph (not in a cell, note, header, footer, text box or caption) whose smallest character size is at least 1.15 / 1.3 / 1.5 times the body size is a level 3 / 2 / 1 heading (`_hangul.SizeHeadings`). The body size is the size most text characters of the top-level body paragraphs are written in (the paragraphs a candidate comes from; not cells, notes, headers, text boxes or captions), counted while they are read, the smaller one on a tie; when the largest top-level size is also the most frequent, holds 40 characters or fewer and is the size of at most two candidate paragraphs (a form or memo with little but its title on top; not a memo's lines all in one size, nor a notice in large print), it is taken for the titles' and the body size is the most frequent of the smaller top-level sizes (`수신 각 부서`, `(앞쪽)`, `[별지 제1호서식]` beside the title), or, with none, of every paragraph but notes (the cells of a form) (`SizeHeadings.body_size`). Known accepted gap: a title whose smallest characters are in the next size (`동의서` with `(안)` in 13 pt) is measured by them. Only text characters count, the same in both formats (`SizeHeadings.count`: not whitespace or controls; HWP skips the 8 WCHARs of a wide control and the paragraph end, HWPX counts the characters of `hp:t`). The paragraph must be at most 80 characters, one line, hold a word (a letter of any script outside `$…$`), not end like a sentence (`…다`, `…세요`, `.` after a word, `?`, `!`; not `…요` alone, which ends 개요, 필요, 주요; not a full stop after a number, `(개정 2024. 1. 1.)`), not end like a sentence inside closing quotes or parentheses (`“…였다.”`, `…한다)`; not inside `」` or `』`, which close law and book titles) or in the polite `…요` form (`함께 해요`, `…죠`; not 개요, 필요, 주요, 중요, 대요), not be wholly in quotes (`"나는 할 수 있다"`), not be a Korean date or time of two units or more (`2024년 12월 31일`, blank `2024년    월    일`, `3월 5일(화)`; a year alone, `2024년`, is a heading of a history page), not be the addressee line of a form (`…귀하`, `…귀중`) or its signature or stamp line (`… (서명 또는 인)`, `… (인)`), not start with a typed bullet or note mark (`ㅇ`, `-`, `·`, `※`, `○`, `▶` …; not `□` or `■`, the section marks of 개조식 reports) or be the caption of a figure or table (`〈그림 1〉 …`, `[표 2] …`, `<표 Ⅲ-1> …`, `[표-1] …`, `〔그림 1〕 …`, `그림 3. …`; a Roman numeral in Latin letters only after `Table` or `Figure`, so `사진 X-ray 판독` is no caption), not hold a web or e-mail address (in ASCII: `서울.kr 소개` is a title) or a Korean phone number (`02-123-4567`, `1588-1234`; not `2023-2024-2025`), and not be a table-of-contents line (text, tab, page label such as `7`, `3-12`, `n+1`). A bullet paragraph is never one. A document with any outline heading (style or head) gets no size headings. The candidates are kept in the blocks as `_SizeCandidate` and settled once the document is read (the body is rendered as it is read, before the body size is known). HWP runs out of order give no size; only the first `length + 1` runs and paragraphs of at most 512 WCHARs (HWPX: 64 runs) are looked at | In 26 real HWP and 8 real HWPX files every paragraph was in the 바탕글 style: chapter titles were 13 pt bold over a 10 pt (or 9 pt) body, and not one heading came out. Without the text guards the same files gave a heading for cover-page dates (`2025.12.12`, `202 . .`), every table-of-contents line, the addressee line of a form and a sentence written large; with them, 196 headings and no such line. Review probes then found more lines of forms and letters printed large that became headings: dates, signature and stamp lines, quoted sentences, polite `…요` sentences, `※ 문의: 02-…` notes, web addresses, and the `ㅇ` items of a 개조식 list set just over 1.15 times its `-` lines (a typed bullet marks a list item, not a title, so `□ 추진 배경` written large is left as text too: a missed heading is milder than a wrong one). Comparing with the size of most characters, not with the size of 바탕글, keeps a document written in 13 pt from turning every paragraph into a heading. Counting the text of tables and notes (written smaller) pulled the body size down, so that every body line of a report in 11 pt with a 9 pt table became a heading; counting HWP WCHARs with their controls gave the same document other headings as HWP than as HWPX. Known accepted gaps: a cover page set in large type gives headings for its lines (`# 도입기업명`); a title in a mixed size (`3. 시험기간 : 시험 시작일로부터`, the label in 13 pt) is body text; the characters of a passed-over `hp:switch` branch are counted for the body size | `test_hwp_size_headings.py::TestSizeHeadings` (including `test_dates_signatures_quotes_and_contacts_are_no_headings`, `test_body_size_is_counted_on_body_paragraphs_only`, `test_hwp_and_hwpx_count_the_same_characters`), `TestSizeHeadings::test_form_whose_body_is_in_tables_keeps_its_title`, `test_titles_that_end_like_a_date_or_a_polite_word_stay_headings`, `test_short_body_lines_over_a_table_stay_body_text`, `test_title_beside_a_shorter_line_keeps_its_heading`, `test_short_lines_all_in_one_size_stay_body_text` |
| Titles by size stand beside outline headings: a document with an outline heading (style or paragraph head) no longer keeps those only (`SizeHeadings.outlined`, removed). Both are headings, the outline heading at its outline level + 1, the title by size at level 1 to 3 | The rule was that an author who marks the structure with outline heads has marked all of it. Measured on the ten documents at hand that have an outline heading (the Korean pairs and the samples of hwplib, hwpxlib and hwp2hwpx), the titles by size it took away were titles in every document that had any and no other line: a request for proposals of 89,000 characters (`hwp2hwpx` `오류/20250418`) has one outline head, on a note, which was its only heading while its 48 chapter and section titles (`Ⅰ. 사업개요`, `□ 보안 요구사항`) were text; a press release whose attachment is outlined (four files of the Korean pairs) lost its headline, which its PDF has as a heading. The five documents whose structure is in outline heads have no title by size and read as before. Looked for again on 75 more press releases of other months before deciding: one has an outline heading (an outline head on one list item), and what the rule took from it was its headline too. In all, six documents lost 53 titles to the rule and no line that is not a title. What stays possible and was not seen: a document whose titles are all outline heads and that sets a line large for emphasis gets that line as a heading, as a document without outline heads always did. Korean pairs MHS 0.759 to 0.776; holdout unchanged; no gated mean moved | `test_hwp_size_headings.py::TestSizeHeadings::test_titles_by_size_stand_beside_outline_headings`, `TestOutlineHeads::test_one_outline_head_on_a_note_leaves_the_titles_by_size` |
| A table of one column in the top-level body, without a caption, is a layout box: a paragraph of its cells (not of a text box or a table inside them) can be a heading by its size too, held to the same text guards (`_size_heading_text`) — but for the headline: a line of a box that ends in the plain style `…다` with no full stop ('K-스마트농업, 중국 시장 공략한다', `_HEADLINE_END`) is a title there, since a Korean press release is titled so in the box its title stands in (5 of 59 releases lost their title to the sentence guard, 4 more of 60 on the holdout), while a sentence with its full stop, the polite style (`…니다`, `…요`) and a question stay text, and outside a box nothing changes — and to level 1 or 2 only — 1.3 times the body size or more (`_BOX_HEADING_MAX_LEVEL`), the body size here being the larger of the top-level one and the size most characters of the whole document have. Headings of one size following each other in a cell are one title set on several lines and are written as one (`# 과기정통부-기후부, AI 데이터센터 신속 구축을 위한 전력계통영향평가 제도 개선 추진`). The paragraphs of each cell are kept as they are read (`_Box`, a `str` whose value is the box as it was written before) and settled with the other candidates once the document is read (`SizeHeadings.box`, `_unfolded`), the box being unfolded by its own table (`TableGrid.unfold`, [core.md](core.md)), which keeps a table nested in it; a box in which nothing becomes a heading is written exactly as before, and in one that holds a heading the text around it is written as the cell of a one-column table is. A cell of a table of two columns or more is still never a heading. Known accepted gaps: a title line that ends like a sentence stays text, so a title of two lines can come out as a heading and a line (`# K-푸드 제1 시장 미국에서` / `‘글로벌 대도약’ 깃발 올렸다`); a table that is one column only once its empty columns are dropped is not looked at | The title of a Korean press release stands in a one-column table above the lines that sum it up. Of 60 releases of November 2026 published as HWPX and PDF (`evals/README.md`), the PDF reader wrote 140 headings and the HWPX reader 11: 47 releases had no heading as HWPX, and the same document had a title in one format and none in the other. The text of a cell was one string by the time the table was drawn as paragraphs, so the paragraph set at 26 pt over a 14 pt body could not be told from the rest. With the box kept: 47 titles, no other heading added, and not a character of the 60 outputs changed besides the heading marks and the blank lines around them; three of the releases that exist as HWP give the same text as their HWPX. Two forms that were tried and measured on the 60: at level 3 too (1.15 times), and with the text of boxes counted for the body size — the second turned five lines of an attachment (`□ (일시) …`) into headings, as the box text outnumbered the body and lowered its size. The rule that a cell is no heading (`test_cell_text_in_a_larger_size_is_no_heading`) was pinned with a table of one cell; it is now pinned with one of two columns, which is what it was about | `test_hwp_size_headings.py::TestTitlesInBoxes`, `TestSizeHeadings::test_cell_text_in_a_larger_size_is_no_heading`, `evals/baselines/korean-pairs.json` |
| A table of one row in the top-level body, without a caption, is a line of text: its cells joined by a space (`SizeHeadings.row`), when no cell holds more than one paragraph, a line break or a table — a row with such a cell stays a table, two texts set side by side not being one line. The line is a heading by its size when every cell is a candidate, by the rules of a box (the row above) and at the smallest of their sizes; and under the label of an attachment in its first cell (`_ATTACHMENT_LABEL`: 붙임, 참고, 별첨, 별지, 별표, with a number or none) and with a second cell it is a heading of level 2 whatever its size. The label decides the bar and not each cell: a cell that could be no heading by its text (a single figure between the label and the title, found on the holdout) does not make it a line. Only HWP and HWPX: the other formats that draw with `TableGrid` have shown no such tables. Known accepted gaps: a row of labels and values ('기관명 \| 한국정보원') loses its cell borders; the spaced label of a bar ('참 고 1') is written as it is set; the PDF of the same document writes the bar as a paragraph | A table of one row is a header over no rows. | A letter-spaced label is written without the spaces between its syllables (`_hangul.unspaced_label`, on each line of a paragraph piece of at most 30 characters without links or emphasis): `담   당   자` → `담당자`, `대   표   이   사` → `대표이사`, a list mark in front kept with one space (`□ 추진배경`). A line is a label only when it is three to six words, each one Hangul syllable, with gaps of the same two or more spaces between them; not when the words are all units of a blank date or time (`년   월   일`, `시   분`), hold the conjunction `및` or `또` (`수  및  양`), are all syllables of one group of a form's choices — weekdays, the parties of a contract, a scale (`월   화   수   목   금`, `일   월   화`, `갑   을   병   정`, `상   중   하`, `대   중   소`); `수정일`, `예정일` mix the groups and are joined, or end in the case particles `을`, `를`, `은`, `는` (`이   름   을`; not 과, 로, 의, which end `검토결과`, `경로`, `협의`). Two such words stay spaced (`성   명`): they are as often the choices or parties of a form (`남    여`, `갑   을`, `유   무`), and joined those could not be undone. One space between one-syllable words is left as written, in a cell too (`기 관 명`, `남 여`, `성 명 홍 길 동`). Only plain spaces are taken out: a tab or an ideographic space separates a label from its value, and each part between is looked at on its own (`성   명<tab>홍 길 동` → `성명<tab>홍 길 동`) | Korean forms space the syllables of a label to fill its cell. In 34 real forms `성   명`, `주        소`, `담   당   자`, `기 관 명` came out spaced, so a search for `성명` or `담당자` did not find them. A first rule (70% of the words one syllable, or both words of a cell) also joined ordinary Korean and the choices of a form, which cannot be undone: `할 수 있는 일` → `할수있는일`, `이 법 시행 전`, `수 및 양`, `약 3 억 원`, cells `남 여`, `상 중 하`, `월 화 수 목 금`, and a label with its value `성 명 홍 길 동` → `성명홍길동`. Lexically `기 관 명` and `수 및 양` cannot be told apart, so the single-spaced labels of the corpus (`기 관 명`) stay spaced, a milder loss than altered text. Splitting on any whitespace joined a label with its value across a tab (`기관명서울시청`) | `test_hwp_spaced_labels.py`, `TestSpacedLabels::test_two_one_syllable_words_spaced_wide_stay_apart` |
