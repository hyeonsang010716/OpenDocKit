# DOCX / PPTX decisions

Deliberate decisions of the DOCX and PPTX extractors (`docx.py`, `pptx.py`) and of OOXML charts (`_charts.py`). Numbering and lists are in [office-lists.md](office-lists.md), equations in [office-math.md](office-math.md), charts (value tables, axes, units) in [office-charts.md](office-charts.md), processing time (work that was not proportional to the input) in [office-limits.md](office-limits.md). Split off from [extractors.md](extractors.md);
the rule is the same: do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change its pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.
