# PDF decisions: fonts

Decisions of the PDF extractor (`src/opendockit/extractors/pdf.py` and the `_pdf_*.py` modules beside it) about characters whose font information is incomplete (widths, encoding CMap) or whose font is a symbol font. Split off from [pdf.md](pdf.md) because of its size limit;
the rules are the same: do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change the pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.

## PDF fonts

| A page where half or more (`_UNMEASURED_RATIO`) of the visible characters have width 0 is read with pdfium plain extraction and a 'structure analysis failed' warning is issued (`_unmeasured`). The copy judgment (`_render_char_few_copies`·`_without_drawing_artifacts`) is not changed. Known accepted gap: that page comes out as line text only, without table or heading structure; and on a page where the zero-width font is less than half of the page's characters, characters of that font may still be lost as before | A font without /Widths for which pdfminer does not know the standard widths either (non-embedded TimesNewRomanPSMT, an embedded font that omitted /Widths) has an advance of 0, so the characters of a line pile up at the same x, and same-place copy removal erased real characters: '2024' became '204', 'report' became 'repot', and word spacing vanished too (round 36). Excluding only zero-width characters from the copy judgment would fail to block pages drawing fake bold or repeated text in the same font (the 110-second, 2.2GB case, pdf-limits.md). pdfium reads with the font's own widths. Standard fonts with known widths (Arial·Helvetica) stay with pdfminer | `test_pdf_fonts.py::TestFontWithoutWidths` |

Some generator writes Descent in units of 1000 (-288) for a font whose glyph space is text space (FontMatrix [1 0 0 1 0 0]); without the range check the descent became -288em and the digits went off the page and vanished without a warning (round 44) | `test_pdf_fonts.py::TestType3DigitsOnALine`, `test_pdf_round44.py::TestType3DescentInTheWrongUnit` |
| If the /Encoding of a CID font is a stream and pdfminer cannot find a predefined CMap by its CMapName, the tables inside the stream (cidchar·cidrange) are read (`_cmap_or_embedded`, wrapping pdfminer `PDFCIDFont.get_cmap_from_spec`; pdfminer unpacks cidchar pairs in reverse order, so `_EncodingCMapParser` reads them, and the range cap `_bounded_cmap_keyword` applies as is). | A character pdfminer reads as U+FFFD takes the character pdfium reads for the same glyph only where the two readers read its whole row alike (`_repair_replacement_chars`, `_repairs`, `_agreeing`). pdfium's characters are its loose boxes, without the ones it makes up; the characters it gives one glyph with one box (a ligature 'ﬁ' read as 'f', 'i') are one glyph; spaces are left out on both sides and texts compared after NFKC. pdfminer's characters are put into rows by vertical centre (within a quarter of the font size, at least 0.5pt, of the row's first character); pdfium's glyphs whose centres lie within that of the row are the row's. A row with a U+FFFD and a decoded character is repaired only when: both readers hold as many characters; paired in the order of their left edges, each pair's left edges lie within 2% of the font size (at least 0.05pt) — or, in a row drawn right to left (turned 180° or mirrored), paired by their right edges, each pair's right edges do — and its centres within the row tolerance; each decoded character reads the same in both; each U+FFFD's glyph is a real character (not U+FFFD, whitespace, a control or a separator); no character of pdfminer has no width; no two lie within twice the edge tolerance of each other; and no other row pairs a U+FFFD with the same glyph. Any mismatch leaves every U+FFFD of the row as it is, as does any failure in the repair (running out of memory still stops the document). The repair changes pdfminer's characters themselves, before pdfplumber builds its characters and the texts of its lines from them, so each line's text holds its own repaired characters; once pdfplumber has built them nothing is changed. Pages turned by /Rotate are left alone, and pdfium is read only on a page with a U+FFFD; the work is the sorts and one pass per row. Shapes that stay U+FFFD: a row with no decoded character (a page number all in old-style figures, a raised footnote figure in a row of its own); a row any character of which the readers read otherwise or place apart — slanted text (turned by its text matrix), copies drawn on top of each other (fake bold), a glyph pdfium reads as nothing, a line of another size that falls into the row of one reader only; and every row of a standard-14 font whose figures pdfminer measures by its own widths and gives no width (glyph names it does not know), as the readers then place the row differently | Book fonts whose ToUnicode map gives U+FFFD for their old-style figures turned page numbers and figure numbers into '�07' and 'Figure 11.1�' (8 of 200 pages of a pdf corpus); pdfium reads them through the embedded font ('207 pages', 'Figure 11.12', 'total 12.', a cell 'Pens | 2'). Vertical writing (Identity-V) is repaired like rows, column by column. With pypdfium2 before 5 (its pdfium measures a glyph turned or scaled by its text matrix otherwise), rows turned 180°, mirrored or scaled by Tz keep their U+FFFD. | The glyphs of a marked-content sequence that says what they stand for (`/Span << /ActualText … >> BDC … EMC`) take that text (`_pdf_pdfminer._stand_for`, in `end_tag` of pdfplumber's device, replaced as `render_char` is): one glyph takes it; up to 8 glyphs on one line (`_ACTUAL_TEXT_GLYPHS`) become one, the widest keeping its place from top to bottom, as wide as all of them, and the others are taken out.
