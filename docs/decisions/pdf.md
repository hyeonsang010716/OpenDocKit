# PDF decisions: extractor core

Deliberate decisions of the PDF extractor (`src/opendockit/extractors/pdf.py` and the `_pdf_*.py` modules beside it): text extraction and fallbacks, scanned pages, ruled and borderless table basics, drawing artifacts, form values. Split off from [extractors.md](extractors.md) because of its size limit; the rules are the same:
do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change the pinning test first and write the reason in the same row. Tests live in `tests/`. Decisions about body flow (reading order, two columns, joining lines, list
numbers) and removal of repeated headers and footers are in [pdf-flow.md](pdf-flow.md) (joining pieces of the same row and lifting a pushed-down line are in [pdf-rows.md](pdf-rows.md); the place of a label beside a table and browser-print superscripts are on the flow side); re-splitting borderless table cells, headers of continued tables, tables inside tables, check boxes and rotated pages are in
[pdf-tables.md](pdf-tables.md) (rows with wide cells, an empty top-left cell and narrow gaps between cells are in [pdf-borderless.md](pdf-borderless.md)); resource limits (time budget, decompression, text-box grouping, rendering), page boxes and attachments are in [pdf-limits.md](pdf-limits.md); pictures drawn clipped, pictures inside table cells and photo grids are in [pdf-images.md](pdf-images.md); fonts without widths and symbol fonts are in [pdf-fonts.md](pdf-fonts.md); joining lines of text in ruled table cells, highlighter marks and tables made of cell shading are in [pdf-cells.md](pdf-cells.md).
