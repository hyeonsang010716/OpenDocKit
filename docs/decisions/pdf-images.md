# PDF decisions: picture placement

Decisions of the PDF extractor (`src/opendockit/extractors/pdf.py`) about where pictures go: the box of a picture drawn clipped, pictures inside ruled table cells, the reading order of multi-column photo grids, and the reading order of text beside a picture and of picture captions.
Split off from [pdf.md](pdf.md) because of its size limit; the rules are the same: do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change the pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.

## PDF picture placement

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A picture's box is the part that overlaps the clipping area (`_figure_boxes`). Clipping that pdfminer and pdfplumber discard (`W`·`W*`, the BBox of a Form XObject) is followed by interpreting the content stream once more, only on pages with pictures (`_ClipInterpreter`·`_ClipRecorder`, together with q·Q). The clipping area is the rectangle enclosing the path points (including curve control points), so it is never smaller than the real area (conservative). Only records whose box equals a pdfplumber picture's box (within 0.5pt) are used, and if interpretation fails nothing is clipped. The background judgment (`_covers_page`) and the scan judgment also use the clipped box | In PDFs that place a photo large and draw it clipped — browser `object-fit: cover`·`overflow: hidden`, cropping in office suites — the original placement box was cropped, so neighbouring body text got mixed into the picture, and if the original covered the page it was filtered out as background and the photo vanished without a warning. pdfminer is not patched globally; the stream is interpreted separately (only pages with pictures; total time over all 4,034 regression PDFs +4%). Besides the reproductions, 4 pictures changed (3 by a 1px difference, and 1 slide where two clipped photos used to cover the body text) | `test_pdf_images.py::TestClippedImages` |
| A picture inside the box of one cell of a ruled table (2pt tolerance, `_CELL_IMAGE_SLACK`) is put in that cell, not after the table (`_place_images_in_cells`): in the cell whose box top and left ends match the start of a row range (`_Table.bands`) and a column range (`spans`), between the lines at the picture's height according to the top coordinates of the cell text lines (`cell_lines`, measured only for cells holding a picture) (at the end if the line count differs from the cell text). For a table with a carried header, a row range without a place is inserted at the top. Tables whose row and column ranges could not be derived, and borderless tables, keep pictures after the table as before | The cells of photo registers and product lists were left empty and the photos piled up after the table, so there was no telling which row a photo belonged to. The other formats put a placeholder inside the cell (assembly folds that row into one line). Putting it only at the end of the cell text sent the diagram in a '산정 로직' cell behind the source paragraph (a real review request form) | `test_pdf_images.py::TestPicturesInTableCells` |
| Three or more pictures (`_PICTURE_ROW_MIN`) placed side by side without horizontal overlap, with top and bottom ends at the same height (within 2.5pt), are seen as one row (`_picture_rows`): only the first (leftmost) picture of the row looks for its place and the rest are put right after it in left-to-right order. Two side-by-side pictures are handled as before (they may be pictures of two columns) | In photo grids of 4 or more columns (product guides, product line-ups) the caption below a photo is wider than half the photo width and looked like 'body text of another column' (`_in_other_column`) for the neighbouring photo, so photos and captions were mixed up. 2- and 3-column grids were read as 'photos → captions' per row, and this was matched. Among the regression PDFs only the 6 reproductions (4·5·6-column grids) changed | `test_pdf_images.py::TestPictureGrid` |
| In lifting pushed-down lines (`_read_lone_lines_in_place`, [pdf-rows.md](pdf-rows.md)) the line to move may have **only a picture** beside it (if a line or table is beside it, it is not moved, as before). A caption line right below a picture (gap ≤ line height) and within the picture's horizontal range (`_is_figure_caption`) also skips lines to skip that have lines beside them (a row of side-by-side cards). The one-column flow judgment (`_one_flow`) is unchanged. The place of a picture moved to another column (`_position_in_column`), when there is no same-column paragraph above it, is before the **topmost** same-column paragraph, not the first one in reading order. Known accepted gap: text on the right that starts just over 1pt above a picture comes before that picture (the second photo of a site report comes after the '2구역' text) | In a layout with the picture on the left and text on the right (a Chrome site report), pdfminer first read the subheadings and the first table column in the left margin as one column, so the text to the right of the picture was pushed behind the subheading and table below (to the end of the page for a clipped picture) — it was not moved because a picture was beside it. A slide's picture caption was pushed behind the row of side-by-side cards below it, and the picture was wedged in at the first same-column paragraph in reading order (a card), ending up between the cards. A right-column line beside a picture on a two-column page stays as is through the condition of vertical overlap on both sides of the gap (`_one_flow`). Of 52 reproduction PDFs only the 5 with text beside a picture changed | `test_pdf_pictures_beside_text.py::TestTextBesideAPicture`, `TestCaptionUnderAPictureBesideText` |
