# PDF decisions: reading order

Decisions of the PDF extractor (`src/opendockit/extractors/pdf.py` and the `_pdf_*.py` modules beside it) about body flow (reading order, two columns, joining lines, labels beside tables, superscripts) and removal of repeated headers and footers (`_remove_repeated_margins`). Joining pieces of the same row ('label value', dot leaders, list numbers) and lifting a pushed-down line are in [pdf-rows.md](pdf-rows.md). Split off from [pdf.md](pdf.md) because of its size limit;
the rules are the same: do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change the pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.

## PDF body flow

| The place of a table or picture is moved into the flow of its own column only when the paragraph chosen by height is body text of another column (no horizontal overlap, and at least half as wide as the table). ## PDF repeated lines (headers, footers, page numbers)

| Decision | Why | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated lines at the PDF page edges are judged per group of pieces at the same height (a row), and rows that group into a borderless table are excluded. Digits are ignored only for single-piece margin lines and pieces shaped like a page number | Judging piece by piece erased the header row and the bottom-row amounts of borderless tables spanning several pages | `test_pdf.py::TestRepeatedMarginsAndBorderlessTables` |
| The margin for PDF repeated lines is relative to the height of the visible area (`_view_box`) (`_in_margin`, shared with the limit judgment `_countable_chars`). Repetition means at least half of all pages **or** at least 2/3 of the odd pages or of the even pages separately (`_repeated_keys`, `_SIDE_REPEAT_RATIO`). Headers that change per chapter are not targeted. Rotated pages use the whole page (`_Page.text_view` None) | In book PDFs the footers inside the trim line were outside the paper-based margin, and the headers of left/right pages (137 of 352 pages) fell short of half. In 35 PDFs the only newly erased lines were running headers and distribution notices. A half threshold per odd/even side erased the titles and signature lines of a bundle of 4-page forms (review reproduction) | `test_pdf.py::TestRepeatedMarginsAndBorderlessTables::test_book_footers_alternating_by_page_side_inside_the_crop_box_are_removed`, `test_margin_line_on_a_minority_of_one_page_side_is_kept`, `test_batched_four_page_forms_keep_their_titles_and_signature_lines` |
| Lines attached at line spacing to a ruled table (outside the margin, `_caption_lines`) and 'label value' rows are removed from the repeated-line candidates. The rule for the two edge rows outside the margin itself is kept. Known accepted gap: repeated body lines at the very top or bottom of a page without a table; a bare number of a 'label value' that is the only row in the margin area | '(단위: 백만원)' and note lines repeated on every page were all erased, and the value of '해지 건수 5' at the end of a page was erased as a page number.
