# Image decisions

Deliberate decisions about images, picture descriptions (the describer) and image placeholders. Split off from the common decision table in [core.md](core.md).
The per-format rules for extracting pictures are in the decision file of each format.

## Deliberate decisions (do not revert)

| When the image limit is exceeded, scanned pages come first. | An 8-bit RGBA PNG (colour type 6) that needs no change is handed on as its own file instead of being compressed again (`image._png_as_it_is`): the long side within `image_max_side` (no downscale, no tiles), no EXIF chunk and no text chunk naming EXIF or XMP (where an orientation is read from), no animation and no frame control (`acTL`, `fcTL`, `fdAT`: before the picture data an `fcTL` alone makes Pillow decode into the frame's box only), no unknown critical chunk, a right CRC on every chunk kept, one `IHDR` and the `IDAT` chunks together (libpng refuses either out of place; Pillow reads a second IHDR), picture data that is one zlib stream with nothing after it (bytes hidden behind the stream were dropped by the rewrite and are not handed on) and that inflates to exactly the rows of the picture (height × (1 + width × 4), the sum over the Adam7 passes when interlaced; inflating stops as soon as it goes past, so 8 MB of zeros after the rows, 8 GB inflated, were inflated whole in 3–10 s and handed on, and are now written anew in 0.05 s), and picture data at most half the raw pixels. An empty `IEND` (a payload inside it was handed on), at most one `iCCP`, before the picture data (one after it, which libpng refuses as out of place, was handed on there; the rewrite writes it before), and one whose profile Pillow read (of two, libpng takes the first and Pillow the last, and both were handed on; one that is not zlib Pillow reads as none, and it was handed on).
