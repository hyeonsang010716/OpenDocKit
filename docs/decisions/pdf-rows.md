# PDF decisions: row pieces and pushed-down lines

Decisions of the PDF extractor (`src/opendockit/extractors/pdf.py`) about joining pieces of the same row (`_join_row_fragments`: 'label value', a value at the end of dot leaders, list numbers) and lifting lines that were read too late (`_read_lone_lines_in_place`).
Split off from [pdf-flow.md](pdf-flow.md) because of its size limit; the rules are the same: do not revert the decisions in this table. To change one, change the pinning test first and write the reason in the same row.

## PDF row pieces and pushed-down lines

| Two PDF 'label value' columns are joined into one line only when they continue for 2 or more rows, the gap between the columns is at most 20% of the page width, and the right column does not continue above or below. If the value column holds only right-aligned numeric values (`_FAR_VALUE`: amounts, rates, dates, page numbers; units such as '원'·'%'·'건' allowed), they are joined even beyond 20% (`_right_aligned_numbers`). Known accepted gap: a row whose label is longer than 25% of the page width (`_TABLE_CELL_MAX_RATIO`) (a question sentence + '답: 예' far to the right) and a distant column whose values are text are left as they are. Single-character Hangul pieces with widened letter spacing are joined together. A conservative criterion so as not to join across a two-column layout. The empty '(경유)' row of an official letter's '수신 / (경유) / 제목' broke the group and labels and values came out separately. Without the numeric-value condition, two side-by-side text boxes ('left 1번째 줄 \| right 1번째 줄', right edges matching too) were joined into one line. In 200 generated form documents about 40 amount groups found their pairs; 0 words lost | `test_pdf.py::TestFlowJoins`, `test_letter_spaced_title_is_one_line`, `test_label_row_with_an_empty_value_between_label_value_rows_keeps_the_pairs`, `test_pdf_forms_and_amounts.py::TestFarRightAmounts`, `test_quality.py::test_pdf_side_by_side_form_xobjects_are_not_interleaved` |

The value-width cap was kept at group level to block groups with only wide values, such as line-numbered documents. | A short value (width at most font size × 10, same size) that ends the row immediately to the right (within font size × 3) of a line ending in dot leaders (5 or more contiguous periods or middle dots, or 2 or more ellipses, `_LEADER_END`) is attached after the leader line (`_leader_rows`).
